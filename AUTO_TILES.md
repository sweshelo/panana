# Automatic map tiles

Kahara (RPG2) and RPG FREE! (lanai) dungeon tile palettes offer **自動** above the manual tiles. The gesture start determines intent: starting outside a room keeps adjacent existing room tiles out of the area test, so a line along a room stays a corridor. Starting on room floor permits expansion using that room as support. Painting a filled area (at least 2×2) within the gesture still creates a new room. The brush updates adjacent corridors, room edges, corners and entrances during the gesture. One gesture is one undo step. A corridor opens an entrance only when the gesture actually paints the room boundary; merely passing alongside a closed wall leaves it closed. Intent is fixed from the original terrain at pointer-down, even after the stroke reaches a room. The normal tile buttons return to manual painting.

`src/editor/autotile.ts` is a pure solver with no game IDs, ROM, UI or document dependencies. It distinguishes full-width room joins from narrow corridor joins and uses diagonal occupancy for inward corners. The controller passes the original tiles and the cumulative gesture cells, making the result independent of pointer visitation order for a fixed start. Only the gesture and its eight neighbours are rewritten; unrelated and unrecognized tiles retain their data.

`src/game/autotile.ts` supplies the Kahara vocabulary. Directions are N/E/S/W; clockwise turns rotate both edge masks and inward corner masks. The mapping uses parts 8 and 13 for single/opposite inward corners, 9 for edge entrances, and 10/11/12 for corner entrances. If the tileset cannot represent a particular topology exactly, the solver selects the closest available shape, prioritizing room joins, then corridor joins, then diagonal corners.

A game opts in by overriding `MapEditState.mapTopology` (the verified profile for the open map) and `autoPalette` (the open tileset's kind → letter indices). The base class offers the brush only when the tileset has a model for every part, and `autoLetter` keeps the preferred variant when the tileset has it. Sharing numeric part IDs does not by itself establish matching geometry, so each game's profile must be checked against its ROM. Section 6 encounter cells are independent of visible room floor and are not changed by this brush.

RPG FREE! uses `LANAI_AUTO`, the same shapes as Kahara (`src/game/autotile.ts`). Facings are decoded by `LANAI_ROT_CODEC` (bytes 4 / 2 / 1 / 8 = turns 0〜3), so the solver works on turns and the tile layout writes the bitmask. Over the 507 outdoor/dungeon maps of the ROM (`test/lanai-autotile.test.ts`), the neighbours of each part fill its wide | narrow edges for 90〜100% of the placed tiles (kind 5 is the lowest at 90%, floors next to empty cells), always more than under any other turn; 8 / 13 lack room tiles on their inward diagonals for 72% / 61% (the rest touch another room diagonally), against at most 6% / 61% under the other turns (13 is symmetric under a half turn). The brush is offered on 495 of the 507 maps. Kind 6 (an unturned floor that only joins other 6s) and kind 33 (empty) are not parts; the brush leaves them alone. The world map (W01) and maps with indoor tiles (kinds 15〜27, which switch MapData to the indoor row) are excluded, as are tilesets lacking any part.

Tests: `bun test test/autotile.test.ts`. The synthetic tests cover corridors, rooms, concave corners, entrance variants, connections between existing rooms, ordering, metadata, map changes and undo/redo. With `roms/kahara-base.cia`, the ROM test also checks palette availability, fallback letter selection and tile serialization.

Room identity follows reciprocal full-width tile openings. Narrow doorways keep two rooms distinct, including directly adjacent rooms. Starting in one room permits expansion using only that room; a destination room cannot become expansion support. Connections at the two room boundaries retain their default entrance tiles.

**部屋のバリエーションをランダムにする** is enabled by default and applies to subsequent automatic strokes. Only floor, edge and corner parts participate; entrance and corridor placement uses the default. Available variant indices receive discrete Gaussian weights `exp(-i²/2)`, centred on the default at index zero. Thus the default has the highest probability. A room has at most one varied floor, chosen nearest its centroid (ties: top, then left). Expanding a room moves this eligible central floor and restores noncentral floors to the default. Samples are cached for the gesture, so pointer moves and redraws cannot reroll a tile; undo/redo restores the actual sampled bytes. Disabling the checkbox does not immediately rewrite existing maps.

`src/editor/autotile-variants.ts` keeps the variation policy independent of game IDs. `src/editor/minimap.ts` uses the same topology to draw reciprocal corridor connections, closed room boundaries, inward corners, and doorway gaps with amber thresholds. Section 6 encounter cells do not define visible rooms. Tests: `bun test test/autotile.test.ts test/minimap.test.ts`.
